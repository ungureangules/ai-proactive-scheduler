I have a business idea that is: A software that uses AI to proactively reschedule recurring clients to services that are recurring. The applications that I already mapped: barbershop, hair saloon, nail saloon, pet shops, and probably some medical fields like dermatologists... 

A good example: I used to cut my hair in a barbershop once per month, but sometimes I forgot to schedule and one more month goes by, and sometimes when I remember the barber did not have availability for my desired date. I know that usually my barber does not have clients all the time. 
The AI will see the client frequency and based on the smallest one (month to month) try to contact the client via message (eg.: whatsapp) offering an available barber schedule in the same or approximately time and the same weekday as the usual for that client.
The client can just accept and the system will schedule it.

The system is a win-win, because the barber is having more clients assigned in vacant hours, the system make the barber more profit by making the customer use the service in minimum time possible more often, and the client benefits for not having to remember when its time to give a boost in the appearance and also very convenient to just reply yes to a schedule specific tailored for him.
